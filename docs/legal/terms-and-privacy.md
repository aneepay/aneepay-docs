# Terms and privacy

The legal documents live on the main site. They are **not reproduced in these
documentation pages** — the hosted versions are the only authoritative text.

| Document | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Terms of Service | <https://aneepay.com/tos/> |
| Privacy Policy | <https://aneepay.com/privacy/> |

The Terms of Service cover, among other topics, eligibility, acceptable use,
API usage, and liability; they state the non-custodial model and link to the
verified payment contract source on the block explorer. The Privacy Policy
explains what personal data the service processes.

!!! note "Keep the link, not a copy"
    If you mirror or excerpt these pages, link back to `aneepay.com/tos/` and
    `aneepay.com/privacy/`. Local copies go stale the moment the site is
    updated — the site always wins.
