# Partner

The AneePay partner program is for websites, communities, and consultants that refer merchants.

## Terms

- Reward: 10% of the platform fee.
- Lifetime attribution; the referral cookie lasts 30 days.
- Commissions become available after a 7-day holding period.
- Payouts are made in DAI to the configured EVM wallet.
- Referral links are generated automatically and include the `?ref=` parameter.

## Getting started

1. Sign up by email and follow the magic link.
2. Set the DAI wallet for payouts.
3. Get your referral link and place it on your resource.
4. Track commissions and payouts in the partner dashboard.
